ADR 0005: Benchmark evidence and comparison methodology

  • Status: Accepted
  • Date: 2026-09-14

Context

Prepared records, the public macro path, formatted output, and external library adapters measure different work. Mixing them into one number produces misleading claims, and short CI runners are not a substitute for a fixed publication machine.

Decision

Keep separate scenarios for prepared-record dispatch, the real public LOGIT_INFO(...) path, formatting, and external-library comparisons. The public macro smoke benchmark may use a passthrough formatter when it is explicitly documented as record-construction/dispatch coverage. Report absolute timings only with compiler, platform, commit, queue, producer, and flush settings; treat CI runs as regression smoke unless the environment is fixed.

Consequences

Benchmark documentation remains comparable and honest across changes. New scenarios require their own workload contract and should not silently replace historical measurements.
