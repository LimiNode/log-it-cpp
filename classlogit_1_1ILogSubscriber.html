Detailed Description

Optional interface for backends that can notify callers when a record is successfully written.

Callbacks receive a LogRecordSnapshot containing the persisted preview and metadata. String fields inside the snapshot are owned copies and may be stored by value. Callbacks are invoked in registration order after the record has been committed to storage. Implementations must not hold internal locks while invoking callbacks.
