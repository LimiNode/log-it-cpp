enum class logit::detail::QueuePolicy
strong

Queue overflow handling policy used by TaskExecutor and SingleThreadExecutor.

Enumerator
DropNewest 

Reject the incoming task when the queue is full.

DropOldest 

Drop-oldest policy; MPSC mode rejects the incoming task to preserve the ordering of already accepted work.

Block 

Producers wait until capacity is available.
