template<class T>
class logit::detail::MpscRingAny< T >

Bounded MPSC ring buffer with arbitrary capacity (C++11).

Template Parameters
TStored type.
Note
T must be nothrow move-constructible, nothrow move-assignable, and nothrow-destructible because cells are published lock-free.
